English vocabulary

remuneration

UK /rɪˌmjuː.nərˈeɪ.ʃən/ US /rɪˌmjuː.nəˈreɪ.ʃən/

noun · formal Money paid for work or services.

Etymology

Prefix
re- back
Root
muner- gift
Suffix
-ation action or result

Latin remūnerātiō (“repayment”) comes from remūnerārī (“to reward”), from mūnus (“gift”).

Usage and synonyms

  • The contract sets out her remuneration for the work.
  • The mechanic received extra pay for working on Sunday.
  • The offer includes a salary of $60,000 plus a bonus.
  • The consultant’s fee is due when the report is delivered.
