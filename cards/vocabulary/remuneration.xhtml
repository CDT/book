English vocabulary

remuneration

UK /rɪˌmjuː.nərˈeɪ.ʃən/ US /rɪˌmjuː.nəˈreɪ.ʃən/

noun · formal Money paid for work or services.

Etymology

Prefix
re- back
Root
muner- gift
Suffix
-ation action or result

From Latin remūnerārī (“to reward”), from mūnus (“gift”).

Usage and synonyms

The contract states her remuneration.

Pay is general, salary is regular pay, and fee is for a service.
